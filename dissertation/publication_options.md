## The honest timing picture

The dissertation is due end of August 2026, and several of the best-fit venues have
**already closed** for their 2026 editions. So the realistic plan is a short abstract or
workshop paper to an open or upcoming venue now, an arXiv preprint as an immediate step,
and a journal for the fuller write-up later. Vini's steer (conference over journal, given
the 13-week timeline) still holds.

## Open or upcoming (target these)

- **GenAIDetect: Workshop on GenAI Content Detection.** The most on-topic venue: its
  shared task is literally machine-generated vs human text detection. The 1st edition ran
  at COLING 2025. A 2026 edition is likely co-located with EMNLP 2026 or AACL 2026.
  Workshop calls usually open in summer with deadlines around August to September, so this
  may line up with our timeline. Action: watch for the GenAIDetect 2026 call.
- **EMNLP 2026** (Budapest, 24 to 29 October, hybrid) and **AACL-IJCNLP 2026** (Zhuhai,
  6 to 10 November, hybrid). The main-conference deadlines are likely mid-2026 (check), but
  the **co-located workshops** (including GenAIDetect and educational-NLP workshops) take
  submissions later, around August. Hybrid means no travel is required.
- **AICS 2026, the Irish Conference on AI and Cognitive Science** (September 2026, Springer
  proceedings). Best location fit and takes short papers. Confirmed by the supervisor at
  Meeting 3 (16 June 2026) to be running this year, with an email received the day before, so
  the event is in September and the proposal window is near. Action: find the call and proposal
  form on aicsconf.org and draft a short paper / abstract soon.

## Immediate step

- **arXiv preprint.** Once there is a presentable write-up, posting to arXiv is free,
  instant, and citable. It establishes the work, can be referenced in the dissertation,
  and is the normal precursor to a workshop or conference submission. This is the quickest
  concrete "result" available.

## Journals (stronger, but slower than the timeline)

- **International Journal for Educational Integrity** (Springer, journal 40979). The
  perfect topical home: it regularly publishes on AI detector accuracy and fairness in
  education. Rolling submission, but review takes months, so this suits the work after the
  dissertation rather than before.
- **Computers and Education: Artificial Intelligence** (Elsevier). Strong fit for AI in
  education, also rolling submission.

## Closed for 2026 (aim for the next edition)

  September 2026, Batumi). Excellent fit (academic integrity, an "ethics of intelligence"
  track), but the abstract deadline was 9 February 2026. Aim for 2027 or another ENAI
  event.
- **BEA 2026** (Building Educational Applications, with ACL 2026). Deadline was 23 March

- **ICNLSP 2026** (Natural Language and Speech Processing, 25 to 26 September). Main
